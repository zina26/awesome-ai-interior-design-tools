## Selection Criteria

Tools are considered for this list when they meet at least one of these
criteria:

- They help users redesign, stage, furnish, or plan real rooms.
- They support room photos, floor plans, renovation previews, or property
  listing imagery.
- They are available as a public product, demo, or documented platform.
- They provide a practical workflow beyond a generic image prompt box.

## Suggested Evaluation Checklist

When comparing AI interior design tools, check:

- Does the tool preserve the original room structure?
- Can you control what changes and what stays fixed?
- Are outputs realistic enough for planning or client discussion?
- Does it support floor plans, staging, renovation, or listing-specific needs?
- Are exports, credits, pricing, and usage rights clear?
